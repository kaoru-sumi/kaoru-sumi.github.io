# Sumi Laboratory

Our laboratory explores affective computing, human–AI interaction, persuasive technology, embodied agents, serious games, and virtual and mixed reality.

## Research Areas

- Affective Computing
- Human–AI and Human–Agent Interaction
- Persuasive Technology and Behavior Change
- Embodied AI and Social Agents
- Virtual, Mixed, and Extended Reality
- Serious Games and Affective Learning
- Nonverbal Communication and Physiological Sensing
