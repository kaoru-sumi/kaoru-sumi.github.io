## Serious Games and Affective Learning

This research investigates serious games and interactive learning environments that integrate emotional, motivational, and social factors into the learning process.

The work examines how narrative, virtual environments, embodied agents, feedback, and adaptive interaction can support learner engagement, reflection, knowledge acquisition, and behavior change.

### Research Topics

- Affective learning
- Serious games for education and training
- Emotion-aware learning environments
- Narrative and interactive storytelling
- Embodied agents for learning support
- Virtual and mixed reality for education
- Motivation, engagement, and behavior change
- Cross-cultural learning experiences

### Research Approach

Interactive systems are designed and evaluated with learners in order to examine how emotional experience and system behavior influence engagement, understanding, and learning outcomes.

The research combines system development, behavioral observation, questionnaires, interviews, and physiological sensing when appropriate.
