## Furhat Social Robot

Furhat is used as an embodied conversational agent to investigate affective and persuasive human–AI interaction.

Our research examines how facial expressions, gaze, head movement, voice, dialogue strategies, and other nonverbal behaviors influence human perception, trust, engagement, and behavior change.

One study used Furhat to investigate how Japanese middle school students responded to deceptive statements generated by an AI-powered robot.

### Research Topics

- Emotion-aware conversational agents
- Persuasive dialogue and behavior change
- Adaptive nonverbal behavior
- Human–AI trust
- Social presence and embodiment
- Cross-cultural human–agent interaction
