## Mixed Reality Agents

This research investigates mixed reality agents that appear and act within users’ physical environments.

The work examines how embodied agents can support learning, social interaction, decision-making, and behavior change by using spatial positioning, gaze, gesture, interpersonal distance, and environmental context.
