## Animal-like Embodied Agents

This research investigates quadruped virtual agents, including dog-, cat-, and horse-like embodiments, for affective and persuasive human–agent interaction.

The work examines how species, body shape, movement, emotional expression, and social behavior influence users’ impressions, trust, engagement, and responses to persuasive messages.

### Current Research Topics

- Cross-species embodiment design
- Emotional expression by quadruped agents
- Persuasive communication
- Social presence and trust
- Movement and nonverbal behavior
- Animal-like agents in VR and MR
- Joint attention and spatial interaction
